## Errata / source changes
- No errors found in the source. Examples are re-created: the book's −16 (opposite of the opposite) becomes −6 so it fits the −8…8 line; the practice quantities use new numbers (250 ft, 900 ft, 6°, $12, $340; 9, −100, 58, −35, 0).
- Added beyond the source (consistent with it): "Stanley is owed $2 → +2" as the opposite of Devin's −2; "0 is its own opposite" (asked in final2).
- Absolute value is ch03: the lesson says "same distance from 0" and counts steps, without naming it.
- The book's "draw a number line from −3 to 3" item is omitted (no drawing question type); its "show the opposite of 2" item is covered by the tap checks.

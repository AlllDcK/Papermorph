## Errata / source changes
- Practice item 3 in the book is −4.8937587253765348728743… with answer "irrational". Finitely many digits can't show irrationality, so it is replaced by 0.1010010001… (one more 0 each time), whose pattern never repeats.
- Book's definition "Integers: all whole numbers (including positive and negative whole numbers)" is phrased here as "whole numbers and their negatives".
- The book's ring colours differ; the lesson uses the engine family hues (BOOK.md).

## Verified answers
4.5̄ = 41/9 rational; 5.6̄7̄8̄ rational; −9.7654321 rational (terminates); −9/3 = −3 integer; 6.675 rational; 2/3 rational; √5 ≈ 2.236 irrational; √4 = 2; 1/45 right of 0; −0.001 and −3 1/8 left of 0.

## Verified answers
- Toy: ψ(s, 1) = −φₓ = 1 − 3s; ψ constant on s − t = const, ψ = 0 where the line leaves through s = 1 (s > t); ψ(0, t) = 3t − 2; p(t) = ∫ₜ¹ ψ(0, τ)dτ = (3t − 1)(1 − t)/2; η = 0 (linear problem), ΔJ = −∫ Δu p dt exactly (checked against the direct sensitivity of J).
- ψ(0; 0,5) = −0,5; p(2/3) = 1/6.
- ũ = −1 on [0, 1/3), u ≡ 1: Δu = −2, ∫₀^{1/3} p dt = −2/27, ΔJ = −4/27 (= J(u*) − J(1)).
- φ = ½(x − z)²: ψ(s, t₁) = −(x(s, t₁) − z(s)).
